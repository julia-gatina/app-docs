DipStreak - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

DipStreak is a tracker for cold showers, plunge tubs and open-water dips. The app itself collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere. The only third party involved is Google, which serves the ads in the app.

Information the app stores

Everything you enter into DipStreak - your plunge sessions (date, duration, water temperature, heart rate, notes), your settings and any in-progress plunge - is stored only on your own device.

  • There is no account, no sign-up and no login.
  • There is no server. We do not operate a backend, and your data is never uploaded to us.
  • We cannot see your data. Nobody can retrieve it from us, because we never receive it.
  • The app works fully offline.

Because your data lives only on your device, deleting the app deletes your data. It is not backed up to us and cannot be restored by us. If your device backs itself up (for example to iCloud), your DipStreak data may be included in that backup, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not this one.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect, transmit or have access to:

  • your name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • your location
  • your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health records
  • usage analytics of our own - the app contains no analytics or telemetry SDK (see Advertising below for what Google's ad SDK collects)

Heart-rate values in DipStreak are numbers you type in yourself. The app does not read them from any sensor or from Apple Health, and they never leave your device.

Advertising

DipStreak is free and supported by banner ads served by Google AdMob.

To serve ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes certain information. According to Google's own disclosure, this includes:

  • your device's advertising identifier
  • your IP address, used to estimate coarse location and for diagnostics
  • advertising data, and data about how you interact with ads
  • crash logs and performance data from the ads SDK itself

This is collected by Google, not by us - we never see it and never receive it.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Tracking and your choice (iOS)

On iOS, the app asks for your permission before any tracking that uses your device's advertising identifier, using Apple's standard App Tracking Transparency prompt.

You can decline. If you do, you will still see ads and the app works exactly the same - the ads are simply less personalised. You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads.

Children

DipStreak is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

  • Access - everything is visible in the app.
  • Deletion - edit or delete individual sessions in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.

There is no request to make to us, because there is nothing for us to hold or delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes will be reflected in an app update.
