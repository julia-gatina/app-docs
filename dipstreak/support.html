Frequently asked questions

How do I start a plunge?

Tap the big start button on the home screen. The timer shows your progress toward your target time and keeps the screen awake while you're in the water. Hit your target or push past it - overtime is tracked too. When you get out, save the session with the water temperature and an optional note and heart-rate reading.

How do I change my target time or water temperature?

Tap the settings row on the home screen before starting. The water temperature you save with a plunge is remembered and offered as the default for your next one.

I forgot to time a plunge. Can I add it afterwards?

Yes. Use the + button on the History tab to log a past plunge for any date, and edit or delete any entry you have already saved.

I closed the app mid-plunge. Did I lose it?

No. A running plunge is restored when you reopen the app, including the time that passed while it was closed. The home screen will offer to resume it.

I left the timer running for hours by accident.

Sessions are capped at 60 minutes - anything past that is treated as a forgotten timer, and the app will ask you what to do with it rather than record an absurd time.

How do streaks work?

A streak is consecutive calendar days with at least one plunge. The Stats tab shows your current and longest streaks, weekly minutes, records and average temperature.

What is the heart-rate field for?

It's optional. If you take your pulse before and after a plunge, enter the values and Stats will show your average recovery. The app doesn't read any sensor - you type the numbers yourself.

Is my data backed up?

Not by us. Everything you enter is stored only on your device. There is no account and no server, which means we cannot see your data - and equally cannot recover it for you. Deleting the app deletes your data. If your device backs itself up to iCloud, your DipStreak data may be included in that backup.

Can I remove the ads?

Not at the moment. DipStreak is free and supported by a single banner ad. On iOS you can decline ad tracking when asked, or later under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; ads still appear, they are simply less personalised.

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely. The only thing that needs a connection is loading the ad.
