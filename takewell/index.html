Takewell - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Takewell: Pill Reminder is a medication and supplement reminder. Your health data - your medications, schedules and dose history - is stored only on your phone and never leaves it. The app itself collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere. The only third party involved is Google, which serves the ads in the app.

Information the app stores

Everything you enter into Takewell - your medications and supplements, their schedules, your dose history (taken, skipped, snoozed), supply counts and your settings - is stored only on your own device.

  • There is no account, no sign-up and no login.
  • There is no server. We do not operate a backend, and your data is never uploaded to us.
  • We cannot see your data. Nobody can retrieve it from us, because we never receive it.
  • The app works fully offline.

You can export your dose history to a CSV file and back up your data to a file at any time. Those files are created on your device and go only where you choose to send them.

Because your data lives only on your device, deleting the app deletes your data (unless you saved a backup file yourself). It is not backed up to us and cannot be restored by us. If your device backs itself up (for example to iCloud or Google's device backup), your Takewell data may be included in that backup, which is governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policy, not this one.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect, transmit or have access to:

  • your name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • your precise location
  • your contacts, photos, camera or microphone
  • Apple Health, Google Fit or any medical records - the only health information in Takewell is what you type in yourself
  • usage analytics of our own - the app contains no analytics or telemetry SDK (see Advertising below for what Google's ad SDK collects)

Your medication names and dose history never leave your device. They are not sent to us, to Google, or to anyone else, and they are never shown on the screens where ads appear to be read by anyone.

Advertising

Takewell is free and supported by small banner ads served by Google AdMob. Ads appear only on secondary screens - never on the Today screen, in the dose flow or in your reminders - and they are non-personalised: we configure Google to serve ads without using your data to personalise them.

To serve and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK still collects and processes certain information. According to Google's own disclosure, this includes:

  • your device's advertising identifier and similar device identifiers
  • your IP address, used to estimate coarse location and for diagnostics
  • advertising data, and data about how you interact with ads
  • crash logs and performance data from the ads SDK itself

This is collected by Google, not by us - we never see it and never receive it. The ad SDK has no access to anything you enter into the app.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Tracking (iOS and Android)

Takewell does not track you, so on iOS it does not show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt: ads are requested in non-personalised mode only, and nothing collected in the app is used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads; Takewell requests non-personalised ads there too.

Children

Takewell is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

  • Access - everything is visible in the app, and you can export your full dose history to CSV at any time.
  • Deletion - edit or delete any medication or dose entry in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.

There is no request to make to us, because there is nothing for us to hold or delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes will be reflected in an app update.
