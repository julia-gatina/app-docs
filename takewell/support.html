Frequently asked questions

How do I add a medication?

Tap the + button on the Today or Meds screen. Give the medication a name, choose the dose and unit, pick a schedule and the reminder times, and save. You can edit everything later from the Meds screen.

What schedules does Takewell support?

Several times a day, specific weekdays, every N days, and cycles such as 21 days on / 7 days off. Medications you take only as needed can be logged with one tap, without a schedule - they still show up in your history on the day you took them.

I didn't get a reminder. What should I check?

Open Settings → Reminder check inside the app. It verifies the phone settings that most often silence reminders - notification permission, exact alarms (Android) and battery settings - tells you exactly what to fix, and can send you a test reminder. The most common causes are battery optimization (Android), Do Not Disturb or Focus modes, and notifications turned off for the app.

What do Take, Skip and Snooze do?

They record what you did with a dose, straight from the reminder notification or from the Today screen. Take and Skip mark the dose; Snooze reminds you again a little later. Tapped the wrong one? Undo is right there on the Today screen.

Can Takewell track my supply?

Yes. Tell it how many pills you have on hand and it counts down as you take doses and reminds you to refill before you run out. When you pick up a refill, use "I got a refill" on the medication to add the new amount.

What happens when I travel across time zones?

Takewell handles time zones and daylight saving for you - your 8 AM dose stays at 8 AM local time, and the app adjusts reminders automatically when the clock changes.

Can I hide medication names from the lock screen?

Yes. Turn on the lock-screen privacy option in Settings and reminders show generic text (for example "Time for your 6:00 PM meds") instead of medication names. Take, Skip and Snooze still work from the notification.

Is my data backed up?

Not by us. Everything you enter is stored only on your device. There is no account and no server, which means we cannot see your data - and equally cannot recover it for you. Use the in-app backup to save your data to a file you keep, and the CSV export for your dose history. Deleting the app deletes your data unless you saved a backup. If your device backs itself up (for example to iCloud), your Takewell data may be included in that backup.

Can I remove the ads?

Not at the moment. Takewell is free - unlimited medications, no subscription - and is supported by a single small banner on secondary screens. Ads never appear on the Today screen, in the dose flow or in your reminders, and they are non-personalised.

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely. The only thing that needs a connection is loading the ad.

Is Takewell a medical device?

No. Takewell only reminds you about the medications you entered and records what you tell it. It gives no dosing advice, checks no drug interactions and makes no diagnosis. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.
