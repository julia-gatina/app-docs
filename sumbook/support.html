Sumbook - Support

Sumbook: Killer Sudoku. Sum sudoku, no ads during play. Need a hand? Start with the answers below, or email us.

How do I play?

A killer sudoku is a sudoku with no starting digits to speak of: instead, the grid is divided into cages, each showing a small sum. The digits in a cage must add up to its sum and never repeat. The app's Learn section walks you through it in five short lessons, and the Cage helper (the Σ button) lists every combination that fits a cage.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Puzzles, hints, the Daily puzzle and your stats all work offline. Ads and purchases need a connection only when you use them. There is no account.

My Daily streak was lost or paused

Streaks are protected against device-clock changes. If your clock is set back, the streak pauses until it is correct again. You get one streak freeze per week, and you can earn extra ones by watching an optional ad.

How do I restore Pro on a new device?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases using the same Apple or Google account you bought Pro with. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

Why do I see ads?

The free version shows a full-screen ad now and then between puzzles (never during a puzzle, never after the Daily). Sumbook Pro removes them for good.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. See the privacy policy.
