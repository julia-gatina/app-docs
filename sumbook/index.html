Sumbook - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Sumbook is a killer sudoku game that works fully offline, with no account. The app itself collects nothing about you and sends nothing to us. Two third parties are involved: Google (which serves the ads in the free version) and RevenueCat (which processes the optional one-time Pro purchase).

Information the app stores

Your puzzles in progress, solve history, streaks, statistics, settings and hint allowance are stored only on your own device.

  • There is no account, no sign-up and no login.
  • There is no Sumbook server. We do not operate a backend and your data is never uploaded to us.
  • We cannot see your data. Nobody can retrieve it from us, because we never receive it.
  • The app works fully offline (ads and purchases simply need a connection when you use them).

Because your data lives only on your device, deleting the app deletes your data. If your device backs itself up (for example to iCloud or Google), Sumbook's data may be included in that backup, which is governed by Apple's or Google's policy, not this one.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect, transmit or have access to your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and contains no analytics or telemetry SDK of our own. (See Advertising and Purchases below for what the two third-party SDKs process.)

When you tap Share on a result, the app builds a picture or text on your device and hands it to the share sheet. You choose where it goes; the app does not send it anywhere itself.

Advertising

The free version shows a full-screen ad occasionally between puzzles (never while a puzzle is on screen, never after the Daily puzzle, never during your first days, and never if you own Pro). You can also choose to watch an optional rewarded ad to earn an extra hint, unlock an older Daily puzzle or earn a streak freeze. Ads are served by Google AdMob.

To serve ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes certain information. According to Google's own disclosure, this includes:

  • your device's advertising identifier
  • your IP address, used to estimate coarse location and for diagnostics
  • advertising data, and data about how you interact with ads
  • crash logs and performance data from the ads SDK itself

This is collected by Google, not by us - we never see it and never receive it.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Consent and tracking choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and similar regions, the app shows Google's consent form (the Google User Messaging Platform) before the first ad is requested. If you decline, the app works exactly the same and simply shows no ads.

On iOS, the app asks for your permission before any tracking that uses your device's advertising identifier, using Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can decline; ads are then less personalised. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads.

Purchases

Sumbook Pro is a one-time purchase (no subscription) made through the App Store or Google Play. The purchase is processed by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. To recognise your purchase when you reinstall or change device, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user identifier and your purchase receipt, and returns whether you own Pro. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Ratings

Occasionally, after you have enjoyed a few puzzles, the app may ask the operating system to show its standard rating prompt. Whether it appears, and what you enter, is handled by Apple or Google.

Children

Sumbook is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

  • Access - everything the app stores is visible in the app.
  • Deletion - delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.

There is no request to make to us, because there is nothing for us to hold or delete. For data held by Google or RevenueCat, use their own privacy controls linked above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes will be reflected in an app update.
